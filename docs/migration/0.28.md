# Migrating to 0.28

From 0.27.x. This page only quotes the notes of the release, and the whole of them are in the [changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md#v0280).

## Does it break?

> Breaking: four props change their name, with no alias for the old one, and four behaviors change.

> The renames are of the name only: the values and what each prop does are the same.

Your code stops compiling until the four names are replaced, and TypeScript shows most of the places.

> TypeScript points at every place: `open` on the `Modal` and on the `ConfirmModal` and `ariaLabel` on the `Menu` are required.

The `position` of a `Tooltip` and the `label` of a `Popover` are optional, so an app that is not type checked, or that spreads its props, should search for them.

## Rename the four props

> Replace `isOpen` with `open` on every `Modal` and `ConfirmModal`, `position` with `placement` on every `Tooltip`, and `label` with `ariaLabel` on every `Menu` and `Popover`.

```diff
-<Modal isOpen={open} onClose={close} title="Edit">
+<Modal open={open} onClose={close} title="Edit">

-<ConfirmModal isOpen={open} onClose={close} onConfirm={remove} message="Delete the item?" />
+<ConfirmModal open={open} onClose={close} onConfirm={remove} message="Delete the item?" />

-<Tooltip text="Help" position="bottom">
+<Tooltip text="Help" placement="bottom">

-<Menu label="Actions" items={items}>
+<Menu ariaLabel="Actions" items={items}>

-<Popover label="Details" content={details}>
+<Popover ariaLabel="Details" content={details}>
```

> The `label` of each item of a `Menu` stays, and so does the `position` of the `ToastProvider`, which is a corner of the screen.

If you import the types of the `Tooltip`:

> the type `TooltipPosition` is gone, `TooltipPlacement` is now the type of the prop, and the four physical sides alone are `TooltipSide`.

## If you style or test the Tooltip by its attribute

> the bubble of the `Tooltip` carries the side where it landed in `data-placement`, as the content of the `Popover` does. It was `data-position`.

> Only a stylesheet or a test of yours that selects the bubble by that attribute has to change.

## If a field sat in a narrow container

> the 200px minimum width of `Input`, `Textarea`, `NumberInput`, `Select`, `Combobox` and `DatePicker` (`--enchase-input-min-width`) no longer goes past the container.

> With room, a field measures what it did.

If you had set `--enchase-input-min-width: 0` to keep a field inside a narrow column, you can remove it. If you read the computed `min-inline-size` of a control, or style the `::before` of the root of a field, check it:

> The computed `min-inline-size` of the control is now `min(<the hook>, 100%)`, and the root of each field has a `::before` with no height that holds the minimum as its intrinsic width.

## If you use the SegmentedControl in a narrow place

> the `SegmentedControl` no longer breaks a label, and scrolls sideways when the options do not fit.

A control that used to grow taller now keeps one line and scrolls. Look at the places where it had little room.

> With `fullWidth`, an option whose label does not fit in an equal share takes the width of its label, and the others share the rest.

If you build your own control with the hook:

> `getGroupProps` of `useSegmentedControl` returns the same `tabIndex`; pass `tabIndex` to `getGroupProps` to override it.

## If you use the Board

> `enchase/experimental/board` changed, as an experimental entry may.

> with `sortable={false}` that menu no longer lists the column where the card already is, and a card with nowhere to go has no menu button.

If the title of a column was a node that also held its description, move the description to the new field, so that the menu shows only the name:

> `BoardColumn` takes a `description`, drawn under the header of the column and tied to it with `aria-describedby`

## What is new, if you want it

> New in the `Button`, `loading` and `loadingText`.

```tsx
<Button type="submit" loading={sending} loadingText="Sending…">
  Send
</Button>
```

> New in `Modal`, `ConfirmModal` and `Drawer`, `initialFocus`: a ref to the element that takes the focus when the dialog opens, which has to be inside the panel.

> New in `Navbar` and `Layout`, `persistentActions`: content that stays in the bar on every screen width, after `actions` and before the theme toggle.

> New in the `CheckboxGroup`, `required`, which means at least one option

> New in the `Avatar`, `size="xs"`

## If you ejected components

> The kit of `enchase eject` changes in 41 files, of 19 components and of the hooks `useFocusTrap` and `useModalBehavior`.

> An ejected component that you leave as it is keeps working with its old prop names; copy it again to get the new ones.
