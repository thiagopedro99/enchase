# Migrating to 0.18

From 0.17.x. This page only quotes the notes of the release, and the whole of them are in the [changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md#v0180).

## Does it break?

> No breaking change.

> The tests of the five components did not change and pass in jsdom and in real Chromium, Firefox and WebKit.

## What to check

> Of the 3,440 renders, 1,040 are identical to the byte, and in the other 2,400 only the order of the attributes of some elements changed: in the buttons of `Pagination`, `aria-disabled` and `aria-current` now come before `class`; in the thumb of the `Slider`, `aria-label` and `aria-labelledby` come after `aria-valuenow`; and in an item of the `SegmentedControl` that has an `aria-label`, it comes after `disabled` and not before `tabindex`. The browser does not care; it shows only in a test that snapshots the HTML of those elements, and such a snapshot changes once.

## If you use the experimental hooks

> The hooks are experimental: they and their types can change in a minor, the notes list every change, and they do not count for the three minor versions in a row without a break that 1.0 asks for.
