# Migrating to 0.11

From 0.10.x. This page only quotes the notes of the release, and the whole of them are in the [changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md#v0110).

## Does it break?

> No breaking change.

> One thing to check if you style the `Popover` yourself: its content box does not scroll any more, a new `popover-body` inside it does (details below).

## If you style the `Popover`

> `--enchase-popover-content-padding-*` still work (they apply to the body), the default look is the same, and CSS of yours that sets `padding` or `overflow` on `[data-slot="popover-content"]` has to aim at `popover-body`.

> When the content has nothing to focus, the focus goes to the body (`tabindex="-1"`).

## If you use the `Table`

> `header` of a column is a `ReactNode` now (it was a `string`; a column that sorts keeps it inside a button, so it cannot hold a button or a link).

## If you use the drawer of the `Sidebar` or of the `Navbar`

> Their DOM is the one of 0.10.0 in the 14 states measured (open, entering, leaving and closed, left to right and right to left), and their CSS and hooks did not change.

> One alignment: a click on the overlay of both now ignores a click that started on a layer above, as the `Modal` always did; it makes no difference with a single layer.
