# Migration guide

One page for each minor version, from 0.10, written when the release is made (the pages of 0.10 to 0.12 were written afterwards, from the notes that those releases already had). A page quotes what the notes of the release say about whether it breaks and about what to check in an app that upgrades, and links to the whole notes in the [changelog](CHANGELOG.md). [VERSIONING.md](VERSIONING.md) says what counts as a break.

To upgrade over several versions, read the pages of every minor in between, from the oldest to the newest.
